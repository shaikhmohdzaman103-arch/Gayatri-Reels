# 01 · START

- **Format:** a portrait montage with kinetic text. Uses only existing assets: the four team portraits in `brand/` and `endcards/01-start.png`.
- **Offer:** Discovery Call · ₹501 incl. GST · keyword **START** (Entry tier: the video removes the risk)
- **Voices:**
  - P = the promoter (Saurav Tandon 2 Pro, `x8QLi04vAqWNOHsZm7yK`). His doubts are spoken over the text cards.
  - G = Gayatri (Devi – Patient & Real Support, `diwmCoikrXSjiTKbIHz9`). The answers are spoken over the portraits as a narrator, not as the person shown.

## Logline

A promoter who has been burned by a consultant before assumes advice costs lakhs, until the first step turns out to be 30 minutes for ₹501.

**Five mechanics:**
1. **Hook:** the private fear of a big bill, shown as text on the first frame.
2. **Number:** ₹501, set against "lakhon".
3. **Reframe:** beat 6.
4. **Conflict:** the promoter's two objections are both answered, and he has no comeback.
5. **Keyword:** START, on the end card.

Every card has 7 words or fewer. The service is named only on the end card.
