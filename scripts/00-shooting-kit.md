# Skit 01 — "Do Saathi" · shooting kit

A 38-second two-person scene. Written for phones, shot by your own team, in one
evening. Divyansh plays the consultant if you want the brand face in it; the two
characters here are the client and his partner.

**Why this is filmed and not animated:** the whole reason this reel works is the moment
Rohit's face changes on the line *"hum numbers hi nahi dekhte."* You cannot draw that.
Two people in a real room will beat any graphic — and it costs you one evening.

---

## The script

| # | Shot | Time | Who | Line |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wide, both at table | 3s | — | *(silence, a clock ticking)* |
| 2 | OTS on Meera | 6s | RAJESH | Chaar mahine se profit nahi. Aur main samajh nahi paa raha kahan gaya. |
| 3 | OTS on Rajesh | 3s | MEERA | Sales toh badhi hai. |
| 4 | CU Rajesh | 6s | RAJESH | Sales badhi hai. Cash nahi. Bank ne CC limit block kar di. |
| 5 | OTS on Rajesh | 3s | MEERA | Toh koi decision galat liya hoga. |
| 6 | CU Rajesh | 6s | RAJESH | Decision galat nahi hota. Hum numbers hi nahi dekhte. |
| 7 | Wide, hold | 2s | — | *(silence)* |
| 8 | Insert, ledger | 6s | RAJESH | Do crore ka maal godown mein pada hai. Pata tab chala jab bank ne statement manga. |
| 9 | CU Meera | 4s | MEERA | Toh har mahine number dekhna hoga. |
| 10 | Card | 3s | VO | Gayatri Business. Har mahine aapke numbers, aapke liye. |

**Total: ~42 seconds.** If you need 30, cut shot 5.

---

## Who plays whom

- **RAJESH** — the promoter. 40s. Not shouting. Tired. He has been carrying this alone
  for four months and is only now saying it out loud. Play him *quiet*, not dramatic.
- **MEERA** — his partner. Sharper, faster, slightly accusatory at first, then the one
  who names the fix. She is not a villain; she is right.

The scene only works if nobody raises their voice. Stress in business owners looks like
flatness, not tears.

---

## The three moments that decide the reel

1. **Shot 6, the turn.** *"Hum numbers hi nahi dekhte."* He says it to the table, not to
   her. Do not cut for two seconds after. Let him sit in it.
2. **Shot 7, the silence.** Two full seconds of nothing. No music swell, no cutaway.
   This is where the viewer recognises themselves.
3. **Shot 9.** Meera does not pitch. She states the consequence. The offer arrives on the
   end card, never in her mouth.

---

## Kit — under ₹0

| Item | What to use |
|---|---|
| Camera | Two phones on tripods, or one phone and one person holding still. 4K, 30fps, vertical. |
| Light | One window at golden hour, or one lamp behind him at shoulder height (backlight). No ceiling tubelight — it flattens faces and looks like an office. |
| Sound | Phone mic at 3 feet maximum. Record room tone for 30 seconds separately. |
| Set | A real office or a real godown office. Not a conference room, not a co-working desk. |
| Props | An actual MIS printout with an ageing sheet. A pen. Two cups of chai, one untouched. |
| Wardrobe | Rajesh: plain shirt, sleeves rolled, no tie. Meera: solid colour, no logos. |

**Framing:** shoot 9:16, eyes on the upper third. Leave the bottom 400px clear for the
caption overlay.

---

## Edit notes

- **Cut on the breath**, not on the word. Let a half-second of silence follow each line.
- **No music for the first 30 seconds.** Add a low pad only at shot 7, and let it lift on
  the end card. Music under dialogue kills the tension.
- **Captions:** Hinglish, white, bottom third, one line at a time. Burn them in.
- **Colour:** warm highlights, cool shadows, slight desaturation. Do not over-grade.
- **Sound design:** one clock tick in shot 1, paper slide in shot 8, silence everywhere else.
- **End card:** logo, "MIS Monthly", comment keyword, WhatsApp. Three seconds.

---

## What will ruin it

- A music track under the first line.
- Anyone smiling at the camera.
- Cutting every 1.5 seconds. This is a conversation, not a montage.
- Shooting in a bright, tidy conference room. The room should feel like work happens there.
- Adding a "hi guys" intro. Start on the silence.
